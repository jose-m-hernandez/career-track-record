# Code of conduct

This repository is a place to talk about a set of tools for checking career
copy. Keep it that way.

- Be civil. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Stay on the topic of the project: its tools, its documents and its code.
- Do not post anyone's private information, including your own records or
  another person's name, in an issue or a comment.
- Do not harass, threaten or demean anyone.

Comments that break these rules may be edited or removed, and the person who
wrote them may be blocked from the repository.

To report a problem with someone's conduct, use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the comment or issue.
