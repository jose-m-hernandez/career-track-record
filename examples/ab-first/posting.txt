Support Operations Lead
Brightfield Goods, remote

About the role
Brightfield Goods sells kitchenware online. We need a support operations
lead to run our help desk and keep customers happy.

What you will do
- Run the help desk and its daily queue.
- Write and improve help articles and reply templates.
- Report on response times and customer satisfaction each month.
- Coach a team of six support agents.

What we look for
- Four or more years in customer support or support operations.
- Experience with a help desk tool and its reporting.
- Clear, friendly writing.
- A habit of measuring what you change.
